# peoplewiki

What the Polkadot products devnet actually does, written down by the people using
it, kept on chain, open to anyone with a mask.

The documentation problem on a young platform is that the accurate parts live in
people heads and the written parts are out of date. This puts the pages somewhere
nobody has to maintain a server for, with edits attributed to masks so you can
see who claimed what.

wrong in public is recoverable; being quietly overwritten is not.
