Every number here is an eth_call to the contract itself, made by this page, in your browser. There is no indexer, no cache and no server in between — which is the point: a monitor that reports what a middleman told it cannot tell you the middleman is the thing that broke.

ContractReadsAnswer

A contract that answers is not the same as a contract that is right, and this page only claims the first. What it catches is the failure that actually happens here: a read that stops returning, a counter that stops moving, a chain that stops answering at all. Growth since your last visit is kept on this device — nothing about you is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it.
