# Third-party content

The six article pages carried by this bundle are taken, unedited, from the
ethereum.org website repository:

  https://github.com/ethereum/ethereum-org-website

That repository is published under the MIT licence, which permits this
redistribution provided the copyright notice and licence text travel with the
copy. They are reproduced in full below.

Every page in this bundle links to its canonical source on ethereum.org at the
top and the bottom, because that is the version that is maintained and this one
is a snapshot. Interactive components in the originals (quizzes, charts, embedded
video, card grids) are not reproduced; `scripts/fetch-content.mjs` reports each
one it removes rather than dropping them silently.
